# Tubarão brand manual

## Identity and setting

Tubarão is a private house-party brand founded by 10 students living together in a house/building in Carcavelos, Portugal. The founders are Master's in Management students, primarily connected to Nova SBE. The community is not Nova-exclusive: guests may come from other universities, Erasmus communities, and wider social circles.

The real house, its large garden prepared for parties, and the original shark painted/graffitied on the garden wall are central to the identity. The project is primarily for the current academic year, not a long-term startup or scalable event company.

Typical parties take place on Sundays, around 18:00–22:30, in the private garden, with open bar included. The initial event scale is approximately 100–120 guests, with possible growth toward 150 only if operations become established. This scale is contextual, not sellable inventory: physical capacity includes residents, as specified in [PRODUCT.md](PRODUCT.md).

Tubarão must feel like a real party born organically in a house in Carcavelos. It must not resemble a nightclub, festival, student association, corporate event company, luxury/VIP club, or fabricated exclusive membership scheme.

## Personality

Raw, underground, coastal, spontaneous, social, confident, playful, slightly rebellious, youthful, authentic, minimal, and imperfect in a controlled way.

The desired feeling is “a genuinely good house party that you heard about through the right people.” Avoid the feeling of a company marketing exclusivity. Raw does not mean messy; underground does not mean overloaded. The approved direction is **minimal + underground**.

References include Carcavelos and the Atlantic coast, house-party culture, early-2000s disposable cameras, real nightlife photography, direct flash, film grain, slight motion blur, imperfect framing, and restrained photocopy/spray texture.

## Name and official logo

The brand name is **TUBARÃO** / **Tubarão**. Preserve the accented spelling where appropriate in brand presentation.

The founders' original shark is the official and primary mark. The supplied project asset is `assets/tubarao-logo.jpeg`. V0.3.1 renders its dark background transparently on the homepage without redrawing the logo. Date/time/location should be clearly readable at a medium size below the headline hierarchy. Do not restore the removed decorative blue heading stripe.

- Always use the original logo. Never invent another shark, redesign or reinterpret it, or replace it with an AI-generated alternative.
- Do not create secondary logos unless explicitly requested.
- Do not over-polish the logo: its slightly handmade/raw character is intentional.
- Make it one of the strongest recurring visual elements.
- It may appear large and centered, cropped at the edges, as a small mark, or subtly integrated into a composition. Its original shape must remain recognizable and unchanged.

## Colour system

| Colour | Value | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tubarão Black | `#0B0B0B` | Dominant backgrounds and large fields |
| Bone White | `#F3F0E8` | Main typography and contrast |
| Atlantic Blue | `#1F5CFF` | Intentional accents |

These three colours dominate the brand. Do not introduce arbitrary additional brand colours. Blue should not overwhelm every screen. Functional colours may communicate successful check-in, errors, or warnings, but do not become additional core brand colours.

## Typography

Headlines should be bold, condensed or strong grotesk/sans-serif, powerful, and poster-like. Uppercase is often appropriate. Acceptable directions include Anton, Archivo Black, condensed Helvetica/Arial-like styles, or similar bold sans-serif fonts. These are directions, not a requirement to install a specific font now.

Body and UI text must be neutral, clean, modern, and extremely readable; Inter or a similar sans-serif is appropriate. Use a simple, strong hierarchy. Avoid decorative fonts. The prototype's actual font stack is recorded in [TECHNICAL.md](TECHNICAL.md).

## Graphic language and motion

Use negative space, strong typography, and the original shark as the foundation. Permitted treatments include subtle film grain, restrained spray or photocopy texture, minimal distortion, understated ocean/wave references, controlled Atlantic Blue gradients, and subtle motion.

Wave and gradient effects must remain sober and subtle. Avoid excessive glitch, waves, or grain; random graphical objects or collage; fake stickers; unnecessary icons; competing textures; and visual chaos. Motion should support the experience rather than delay purchase or compete with the content.

## Photography

Prefer real guests at real Tubarão events. The intended style is early-2000s house-party/disposable-camera photography: direct flash, grain, slight motion blur, candid moments, occasional wide angles, imperfect framing, and the nighttime garden.

Do not use AI-generated strangers as fake Tubarão guests or fill mockups with random people. Until real event photography exists, use typography, the logo, colour, texture, and abstract/minimal compositions. Real party content should become the preferred material as it becomes available.

## Higgsfield and AI production

Higgsfield may later act as a creative production layer; it does not define the identity. Potential uses include animation of the existing logo, subtle hero motion, Instagram Stories, Tickets Live / Few Tickets Left / Sold Out graphics, teasers, Reel covers, motion graphics, transitions, recap treatments, and social variations.

AI should enhance the established identity without replacing it. Logo animation must respect the original mark rather than redesign it. Do not generate fake attendees. These potential uses do not authorize asset generation or implementation now.

## Voice and content rules

All public-facing copy is **English**. The voice is short, confident, informal, simple, direct, and slightly playful. The brand should not take itself too seriously. Internal/admin technical labels may also use English.

Approved examples:

- TICKETS LIVE.
- THIS SUNDAY.
- FEW TICKETS LEFT.
- SOLD OUT.
- YOU'RE IN.
- SEE YOU SUNDAY.
- SEE YOU IN THE GARDEN.
- DON'T BE LATE.
- 18:00.
- TUBARÃO.

Avoid long marketing copy, “We are delighted to announce…”, “Join us for an unforgettable exclusive experience…”, “VIP”, “Premium experience”, “Apply now”, and “Exclusive members only”. Do not use application/approval language such as “Apply”, “Request Access”, “Membership Application”, “Approval Required”, or “VIP Access”.

Use a simple follow instruction and self-confirmation: “Follow Tubarão”, “Open Instagram”, and “I follow @casitatubarao”. The official URL is https://www.instagram.com/casitatubarao/. The handle, URL, and follow instruction are intended to be configurable.

Present one clean final ticket price, without a separate payment-fee line unless explicitly required later. Public availability is qualitative: AVAILABLE, FEW TICKETS LEFT, or SOLD OUT. Never publish exact physical capacity, category availability, or remaining inventory.

Display the category rule clearly during purchase:

> Please select the correct ticket category. Tickets purchased under the wrong category may be cancelled and refunded.

## Website experience

Design mobile first for visitors arriving from Instagram. The public flow should be extremely fast, with a prominent original logo, event date/time, open bar, minimal information, and generous negative space.

Avoid complicated navigation, unnecessary menus, guest accounts/passwords, lengthy FAQs, About Us pages within the primary purchase flow, extra forms, unnecessary animation, and ecommerce-style complexity. The site should feel like Tubarão rather than a SaaS product. Hide operational complexity while preserving clear purchasing instructions.
